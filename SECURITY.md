# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Email **work@rvastudio.in** or use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/aby050/kitnap/security/advisories/new).

Include steps to reproduce, the app version and platform, and the impact. You can expect an
acknowledgement within 72 hours and a fix or mitigation plan within 14 days.

## Scope

Of particular interest:

- bypassing a block, schedule or guardian lock without the intended cost,
- unlocking paid features without a valid purchase,
- any data leaving the device other than through the store / RevenueCat purchase flow.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release on the App Store and Google Play receives security fixes.
