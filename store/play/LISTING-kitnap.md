**Title (30):** Kitnap: App Blocker & Focus
**Short description (80):** Block distracting apps, cut screen time and sleep better. A calm digital detox.

**Full description:**
Kitnap is a calm app blocker that helps you put the phone down and get your time back. Block the apps that pull you in, cut your screen time, focus on what matters and sleep a little better.

Kitnap is made in memory of Biscuit, a little cat who never needed a screen to be happy: just a sunny spot, a long nap and someone beside him. It is here to help you rest your eyes, quiet your mind and look up for the ones right next to you.

BLOCK DISTRACTING APPS
Pick the apps that steal your day — social media, short videos, games, news, anything — and Kitnap locks them. When you reach for a blocked app, Kitnap covers it before the scroll starts. Choosing apps is simple: tap an app and it lights up in its real colours.

ONE CALM WAY BACK IN
Need an app for a moment? Answer a short question, solve a quick sum or take a slow breath first. That small pause is often all it takes to change your mind. No workouts, no camera, nothing to fight with.

FOCUS SESSIONS THAT HOLD
Start a block for 30 minutes or for days. While it runs, the apps stay closed — no pausing, no waiting it out — while a calm scene plays in the background. Great for study, deep work, reading and family time.

SCHEDULES FOR SLEEP AND FOCUS
Set apps to lock on their own: late at night for better sleep, first thing in the morning, through the working day or at dinner. Kitnap builds your first schedule from the time your phone usually wins.

SEE WHERE YOUR TIME GOES
Your real screen time, the apps that take the most, your streak and the hours you have taken back — from your phone's own usage data. No made-up numbers.

A FRIEND CAN HOLD THE KEY
Make someone you trust your guardian. Loosening your blocks then needs their code, so you cannot quietly undo them at 11pm.

ALWAYS SAFE
Calls, messages, maps, payments and emergency access are never blocked. Bailouts let you end a locked session early if you truly need to.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed and nothing leaves your phone. Kitnap uses Android's Accessibility Service only to notice when a blocked app opens so it can show the block screen. It never reads what is on your screen.

KITNAP PRO
Kitnap is free, and the free version really blocks apps. Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that adds more bailouts, the guardian code and unlimited schedules. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Less scrolling. More living.
